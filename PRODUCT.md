# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary visitors are HR professionals and learning-and-development leaders evaluating corporate training for teams in high-pressure workplaces. Their main job is to assess program fit, credibility, format, and investment, then book a corporate discussion.

Workshop participants are corporate professionals who need practical ways to manage stress, attention, emotions, and performance at work.

## Product Purpose

Bombay Academy of Management Studies helps organisations build resilient, focused, high-performing teams through onsite corporate mind-management and human-performance workshops. The website succeeds when a qualified HR or L&D buyer understands the offer and initiates a workshop inquiry.

## Positioning

BAMS applies management-training rigour developed since 1986 to practical mind management, combining corporate experience with evidence-based stress management, breathwork, meditation, emotional regulation, and performance techniques.

## Operating Context

Buyers evaluate programs for groups of 10–20 participants, choose a full-day workshop or two-hour experiential session, and discuss onsite delivery at their premises or another venue in India. Programs can be customised to organisational needs.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Current programs are:

- Mind Mastery for Peak Performance — full day
- Stress Management & Nervous System Reset — full day
- Time Management & Mental Focus — full day
- Meditation for Corporate Wellness — two hours

The standard full-day onsite workshop costs ₹50,000 for up to 10 participants, plus ₹5,000 for each additional participant up to 20. Custom programs are available on request.

The website is a static Astro site. Contact and social destinations are not yet connected in the current implementation.

## Brand Commitments

The full name is Bombay Academy of Management Studies, abbreviated BAMS. It was founded in 1986 by Shri R. K. Rawat and is now led by Archana Rawat. Preserve the established BAMS logo and a credible, practical, corporate voice. Programs are presented as rigorous, experiential, non-religious, and designed for workplace use rather than generic wellness content.

## Evidence on Hand

Approved site content includes founder and leadership credentials, the current workshop portfolio and pricing, the legacy training portfolio, and named client relationships. Named clients include Siemens, Pidilite Industries, Indian Oil Corporation, Bharat Petroleum, BEST Mumbai, BGR Energy Systems, Doosan Infracore India, Coromandel Engineering, Nagarjuna Oil Corporation, and Express Publications.

The logo is currently hosted at `https://ik.imagekit.io/violetviolinist/bams/logo.png`. Local favicon assets are in `public/`. Testimonials and insights are explicitly not yet available and must not be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Lead with practical value for HR and L&D buyers.
- Connect current mind-management programs to BAMS's management-training legacy.
- Make format, audience, outcomes, and investment easy to evaluate.
- Support claims with approved credentials, client history, and concrete program details.
- Never invent testimonials, research evidence, clients, or performance outcomes.
